Total Dynamic Head (TDH) Calculation

In closed-loop HVAC systems, the pump only needs to overcome friction losses — not elevation. Static head cancels out in a circulation loop.
H = R × L × ZF / 10,000

Where:
• R = Pipe friction loss (Pa/m) — 50 to 150
• L = Pipe length (m) — feed + return
• ZF = Fitting addition factor (1.0 to 2.0)
• 10,000 = Conversion factor (Pa → m H₂O)

Source: VDMA 24199 / Wilo Pump Basics Guide

Flow Rate Calculation

Q = Φ̇ / (ρ × cₚ × ΔT)

Where:
• Φ̇ = Heat flow rate (W / kW)
• ρ = Water density ≈ 1000 kg/m³
• cₚ = Specific heat ≈ 4182 J/(kg·K)
• ΔT = Temperature spread (K)

Simplified: Q (m³/h) = Φ̇ (kW) × 0.86 / ΔT (K)
