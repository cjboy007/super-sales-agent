ErP 641/2009

Requirement: EEI ≤ 0.23 for all circulator pumps

Hero Pump: Estimated EEI ≤0.16 (testing in progress)

Test Standard: EN 16297-1/2/3:2012

Status: 🟢 Compliant (estimated), formal test pending

Testing strategy: Domestic pre-test (¥15-40K) → EU formal test at DTI Denmark (€3-8K/model)

BACS Directive

Requirement: Building Automation & Control for HVAC >290kW (now) / >70kW (2030)

Hero Pump: Modbus RTU standard → BACS Class B compliant

Competitor Gap: Grundfos/Wilo require €100-200 add-on module

Status: 🟢 Compliant out of the box

EPBD Recast

Deadline: May 29, 2026

Impact: Building Renovation Passport mandatory → more retrofits → more pump replacements

Opportunity: Every retrofit needs a new pump. Hero Pump is priced for volume replacement.

EC already launched infringement procedures against lagging member states

Timeline: Commission report June 30, 2026 → mandatory law ~June 2027

Key: SRI is about building rating, not pump rating. Hero Pump enables higher scores.
