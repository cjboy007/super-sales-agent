## Style Prompt

Super Sales Agent marketing video uses the existing SSA cockpit identity: a dark export-sales command center, dense but legible operational panels, precise status badges, and restrained motion. The visual tone is technical, trusted, and commercially serious. The video should feel like a working sales room, not a generic AI SaaS launch.

## Colors

- Background: `#050916` near-black cockpit canvas.
- Panel: `#0f172a` deep slate surfaces.
- Panel border: `#273449` low-contrast structure.
- Foreground: `#e5edf8` primary text.
- Muted: `#8ea0ba` secondary labels.
- Emerald: `#10b981` approved, live, safe actions.
- Amber: `#fbbf24` pricing risk, review, deal urgency.
- Blue: `#60a5fa` workflow and navigation signals.
- Red: `#f87171` competitor and risk warnings.
- Purple: `#a78bfa` AI/analysis accent.

## Typography

- Display and body: `IBM Plex Sans`, with strong 800-900 headlines and light 300-400 supporting copy.
- Data and operational labels: `Geist Mono`, using tabular numerals.

## Motion

- Medium energy, cockpit-polished, no jump cuts.
- Primary transition: smooth crossfade plus a small directional reveal.
- Elements enter with mixed y, scale, and opacity changes, then remain visible until the next scene covers them.
- Screenshot motion is slow zoom/pan only, as if reviewing a live control room.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make it a broad "AI agent" hype reel.
- Do not use purple-blue gradients as the main identity.
- Do not use stock illustrations, mascot art, or decorative blobs.
- Do not show uncontrolled auto-send behavior.
- Do not make the UI look empty or toy-like; keep it operational and evidence-rich.
