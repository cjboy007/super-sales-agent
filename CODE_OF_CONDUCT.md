# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, and outcomes.
- Assume public project spaces are shared professional spaces.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive comments that derail technical discussion.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project community.

Report concerns through the maintainers' private contact channel.
